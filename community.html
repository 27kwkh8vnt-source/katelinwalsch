Community Work.

Davis Polk Leadership Initiative

Innovation Grant Recipient · Columbia Law School

As a recipient of the Davis Polk Leadership Initiative Innovation Grant, I received $1,000 to develop a project designed to strengthen community at Columbia and build connections with the surrounding Morningside Heights and Harlem communities.

I organized a service event bringing together Columbia faculty and support staff to assemble care packages for unhoused members of the surrounding community. The project was designed both to foster relationships among colleagues across the university and to create an opportunity to serve the broader community together.

In partnership with the Columbia Law School Legal Outreach Team, participants assembled more than 250 care packages containing essentials including hygiene and sanitary products, hand warmers, and snacks for distribution to unhoused people in Harlem and Morningside Heights.

Justice-in-Education Initiative

Lead Workshop Coordinator · Columbia University

November 2023 – May 2025

Through Columbia University's Justice-in-Education Initiative, I volunteered on Rikers Island, where I led a weekly book club for incarcerated women participating in substance use recovery programming.
